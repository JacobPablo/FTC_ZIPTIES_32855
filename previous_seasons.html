Previous seasons

This section covers our experiences and developments across various FTC seasons.

2025–2026 Season

During the DECODE season we built a few iterations that eventually led to our final design. Our robot was at first based off the goBILDA starterbot, but thanks to rapid prototyping we were able to develop more-efficient and accurate iterations. We also developed custom 3D-printed parts in CAD to enhance our robot's performance. Some benefits of this approach included:

  • Faster development time: We were able to quickly test and iterate on our designs, allowing us to identify and fix issues early on.
  • Optimization of tasks: By utilizing 3D printing, we were able to optimize our robot's performance for specific tasks, such as scoring, navigating the field, and loading artifacts without human intervention.

This season we participated in the Benelux Qualifier in Helmond and achieved a top 15 finish. In regards to outreach, we applied for and received a subsidy from Rabobank. To attract more students to our team, we did the following:

  • Participated in school open days to promote our team and showcase our robot.
  • Sent emails to students and hung up flyers to inform and invite them to our team.
